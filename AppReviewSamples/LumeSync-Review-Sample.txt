Harbor Light

At the edge of a quiet harbor, a lighthouse turns above the water. Its beam
crosses the old pier, passes over a line of empty boats, and fades into the
open sky. A bell rings once near the channel marker.

Inside the tower, a brass clock measures each minute. The keeper checks the
weather log, adjusts the lamp, and watches the horizon. Clouds move slowly from
west to east. Below, small waves rise and settle against the stone wall.

Near dawn, the sky changes from deep blue to silver. The harbor becomes clear,
the last stars disappear, and the lighthouse completes one final turn before
the morning begins.
