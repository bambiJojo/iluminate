# Physical-device recording for build 10030

Use a physical iPhone or iPad with LumeSync 1.0 (10030) installed once available in TestFlight.

1. AirDrop `AppReviewSamples/LumeSync-Review-Sample.m4a` from this project to the device and save it in Files. This is a verified regenerated copy of the original synthetic narration; see `AppReviewSamples/PROVENANCE.md`. Alternatively, download and unzip the existing App Review attachment `LumeSync-1.0-App-Review-Samples-v2.zip`.
2. Complete onboarding. In Library, use **+ > Import from Files** and select `LumeSync-Review-Sample.m4a` (or another authorized, clearly audible audio file).
3. Set speaker volume high enough to hear narration. Start a screen recording, open the imported item and tap Play.
4. After narration becomes audible, navigate to the **Home Screen** and leave it visible for 15–20 seconds while audio continues.
5. Open Control Center and demonstrate pause/resume and a 15-second skip. Return to the app and stop playback, then end the recording.
6. Play the saved recording and confirm it contains audible narration both before and after going Home. If internal sound was not captured, re-record with an appropriate audio capture method; a silent video does not demonstrate Apple's requirement.
7. Save the verified video to the Mac and provide its local path for attaching to App Review Information. Do not claim the recording is attached until its upload is verified.

Separately test screen locking, an interruption and a headphone/speaker route change. These checks require real hardware; simulator tests do not establish background-audio compliance.

App Review also needs the same recording attached for future submissions, as requested in the rejection.
